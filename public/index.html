A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

What kind of design work do you do?+

Logos and brand identities are my main focus. I also design jerseys, social media banners, campaign visuals, and print and packaging artwork.

What do you need to start my project?+

Tell me about your brand, audience, what you need, and your deadline. Include preferred colors or reference links in your message. We can arrange a way to share existing logos or other files afterward.

How long will my design project take?+

Timing depends on the scope and number of designs. I’ll confirm a realistic delivery schedule after reviewing your brief and before we begin. If you have a fixed launch date, include it in your message.

Can I request changes to the design?+

Yes. We’ll agree on the revision scope before starting so you know what’s included. For Fiverr orders, revisions follow the package you choose. New concepts or extra deliverables can be discussed separately.

Which files will I receive?+

We’ll agree on the files you need for your project, such as transparent PNGs, JPGs, vector files, or print-ready PDFs. Editable source files and any production requirements should be confirmed in the brief or Fiverr package.

Can I work with you through Fiverr?+

Yes. You can view my Fiverr gig ↗ and choose a suitable package. For a custom request, send your brief first so we can agree on the scope, price, and timeline.
